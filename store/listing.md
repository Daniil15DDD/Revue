# Google Play listing

**App name** (30 max): Revue: Shows, Anime & Books

**Short description** (80 max):
Save links, track shows, anime and books, and ask an AI that runs on your phone.

**Full description** (4000 max):

Revue is one home for everything you watch, read and save.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
Share a link from YouTube, Chrome or any app straight to Revue. It fills in the title and preview picture for you. Paste a link, write a thought, or import screenshots.

FIND A SHOW OR BOOK IN ONE TAP
Search series, anime and books and add them with the status you want: Plan to watch, Watching, Watched, On hold or Dropped (or the reading versions for books).

PROGRESS THAT FINDS ITSELF
Revue looks up how many episodes, seasons or pages something has. Then it's one tap for the next episode, or set the page you're on.

YOUR LISTS, YOUR WAY
Make your own lists, give each a colour or your own cover photo, and group them into folders like Watching or Reading.

STATS
See what you saved this week, month or year, what's in progress, how many episodes you've watched and pages you've read.

SCREENSHOTS YOU CAN SEARCH
Text in your screenshots is read on your phone, so you can find them later.

ASK REVUE
Download a small AI model once, then ask questions about your library, like "what was that sci-fi book I saved?" It runs entirely on your phone, even offline.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your library stays on your phone. Revue only goes online for link previews, catalogue lookups and the one-time model download.
