# Revue Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 October 2026_

Revue is a free app for saving links, screenshots, shows, anime and books. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics of our own and no server. **We, the makers of Revue, never receive your data.**

## What stays on your phone

Everything you put into Revue is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

- items you save (titles, links, notes, ratings, status and progress)
- your lists, folders and cover photos
- screenshots and photos you import, and the text read from them
- the AI model, if you download one

The text in screenshots is read by Google ML Kit **on the device**. The AI assistant and auto-tagging run on a model that is **on the device**. Neither sends your content anywhere. Conversations with the assistant aren't saved at all; they're gone when you leave the screen.

## When Revue goes online

Revue connects to the internet only to do what you asked. Each request goes straight from your phone to the service below; none of it passes through us. Like any website you visit, these services can see your IP address and the request itself.

| What you do | What is sent | Sent to |
|---|---|---|
| Save or share a link | The link (to fetch its title and preview image) | The website the link points to; for YouTube links, YouTube's oEmbed service |
| Search for a show, anime or book | Your search text | TVmaze, AniList, Open Library |
| Add a show, anime or book | Its title (to find episode, season or page counts and the cover) | TVmaze, AniList, Open Library, or AniLiberty for AniLiberty links |
| Download the AI model | A download request | Hugging Face |
| Open a saved link | The link | Your browser or the app that opens it |

These services have their own privacy policies:
[TVmaze](https://www.tvmaze.com/site/privacy) ·
[AniList](https://anilist.co/terms) ·
[Open Library / Internet Archive](https://archive.org/about/terms.php) ·
[YouTube / Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) ·
[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/privacy).

## Google ML Kit

Revue uses Google ML Kit to read text in screenshots. ML Kit does this on the device and does not send your images or their text to Google. Google says ML Kit may contact its servers for updates and send **performance and usage metrics** (for example device model, app version and how the feature performed) to Google. See [Google's ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure).

## What we don't do

- We don't collect, store or sell personal data.
- We don't use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.
- We don't ask for your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Revue sees a photo only when you pick it or share it to Revue.

## Deleting your data

Your data is on your phone, so you're in control of it:

- Delete a single item, list or folder in the app.
- Delete everything: **Settings → Erase All Data**.
- Remove the AI model: **Settings → On-device AI model → Remove**.
- Uninstalling Revue deletes all of its data.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children

Revue is not directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
